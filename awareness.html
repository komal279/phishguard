Phishing Awareness

Learn, Detect, Protect

🎣

What Is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyberattack that tricks users into clicking deceptive links or revealing sensitive information.

📩

Email Phishing

Attackers impersonate banks, employers, or authorities to steal credentials using fake correspondence.

📱

Smishing

SMS-driven phishing tricks users with urgent texts and fake verification links.

📞

Vishing

Voice phishing relies on social engineering through phone calls to extract personal details.

🎯

Spear Phishing

Highly targeted messages use personal details to appear credible and convincing.

🔐

Prevention Tips

Never click unknown links, verify the source, enable multi-factor authentication, and review URLs carefully.
